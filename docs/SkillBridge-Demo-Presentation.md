# SkillBridge --- Demo Presentation

**Suggested duration:** 5--7 minutes

## 1. What is SkillBridge? (45 seconds)

SkillBridge is a collaboration platform where people can discover
projects, find teammates, and turn ideas into real working projects.

Instead of learning or building alone, users can join projects that
match their interests and skills---or create a project and invite others
to contribute.

## 2. Who is it for? (45 seconds)

SkillBridge is designed for:

- Students and beginners who want practical experience.
- Developers, designers, and other creatives looking to collaborate.
- Project owners who need people with specific skills.
- Anyone who wants to build their portfolio through real teamwork.

## 3. The problem we are solving (1 minute)

People often have ideas but struggle to find the right collaborators. At
the same time, many learners want practical experience but do not have
an easy way to find projects where they can contribute.

This can leave people learning in isolation, struggling to build a
portfolio, or unable to move an idea forward because they lack a team.

## 4. Our solution and why it matters (1 minute)

SkillBridge brings project discovery and team collaboration into one
place.

Users can publish project ideas, describe the roles and skills they
need, discover opportunities, and apply to join teams. Project owners
can review applications and manage their projects.

We believe this solution is worth building because it helps connect
learning with practical experience. It gives people a place to
contribute, learn from others, demonstrate their abilities, and make
progress on meaningful projects.

## 5. Key features to demonstrate (2--3 minutes)

1. **User accounts and profiles** --- Register, log in, and create a
    profile with skills, interests, and links.
2. **Explore projects** --- Browse projects and search or filter
    opportunities.
3. **Create a project** --- Publish an idea with its description,
    technology stack, and roles needed.
4. **Apply to join** --- View project details and submit a join
    request.
5. **Owner dashboard** --- View owned projects and review applications.
6. **Applicant dashboard** --- Track submitted applications and their
    statuses.
7. **Account settings** --- Manage appearance preferences and account
    actions.

## 6. Closing (30 seconds)

SkillBridge is about making collaboration easier and helping people grow
by building together.

Our goal is simple: **connect people, skills, and ideas so more projects
can move from concept to reality.**

Thank you.
